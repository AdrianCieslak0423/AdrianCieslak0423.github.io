RC Car
Designed and assembled a custom 2WD RC car targeting a top speed of 30 MPH, integrating mechanical, electrical, and drivetrain systems while performing component selection and performance calculations. Testing identified drivetrain material limitations under real-world loads, leading to a redesign recommendation using tougher gears to achieve the intended performance.

- Mechanical System Design
- 3D Printing
- Failure Analysis & Troubleshooting
- System Testing & Validation
- SOlidWorks
- Assembly
